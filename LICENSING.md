# Strata RMM Licensing

Strata RMM uses a dual-license model.

## Community Edition

Community Edition is open-source software under
`AGPL-3.0-or-later`. Network operators that modify it must offer the
Corresponding Source to users interacting with the modified service, as
required by AGPL section 13.

Community interfaces include a subtle persistent “Powered by Strata RMM” legal
link. The legal view identifies the copyright holder, warranty status, license,
and Corresponding Source location. Reasonable legal notices and author
attributions must be preserved to the extent allowed by AGPL section 7(b).

The AGPL grants rights to use, study, modify, and redistribute the covered
software. It does not grant trademark rights or imply endorsement.

## Enterprise License

An executed Enterprise agreement may provide:

- private modifications without AGPL source-disclosure obligations for the
  commercially licensed release;
- supported white-label presentation;
- commercial deployment rights and negotiated support;
- enterprise-only integrations, policy controls, or service levels;
- trademark permissions stated in the signed agreement.

Repository possession, an environment variable, or a UI setting does not prove
an Enterprise license. Until a verifiable commercial entitlement is wired into
runtime configuration, the application must operate as Community Edition and
retain the legal attribution.

`COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md` is a product summary, not a grant of rights. Commercial
rights exist only through a signed agreement issued by Strata Development
Platform.

## Contributions

Before accepting outside contributions, adopt a contributor agreement or
inbound licensing policy that preserves the project’s ability to offer the same
code under both AGPL and commercial terms. Do not merge third-party code under
terms incompatible with either distribution.

## Legal review

This repository describes the intended licensing model but is not legal
advice. Counsel should approve the copyright ownership chain, contributor
terms, trademark policy, attribution language, and Enterprise agreement before
commercial launch.
